{{ site }} answers one question quickly: will this processor and this graphics card work well together? We maintain a small, hand-checked database of current desktop CPUs and GPUs and publish a bottleneck estimate for every pairing at 1080p, 1440p and 4K, plus a power-supply recommendation.

The site is independent. We do not sell hardware and we are not paid by any manufacturer. It is funded by display advertising.
