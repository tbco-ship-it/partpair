Connectors

{% if gpu.tdp >= 300 %}Cards in this power class use the 12V-2x6 (12VHPWR) connector or three 8-pin PCIe connectors depending on the board partner. If yours has the 16-pin connector, a power supply with a native 12V-2x6 cable avoids adapters.{% elif gpu.tdp >= 200 %}Expect two 8-pin PCIe connectors or a single 12V-2x6 depending on the model. Most 650–850 W units from the last five years have both.{% else %}A single 8-pin (or 6+2-pin) PCIe connector is typical; some low-power models draw everything from the slot. Almost any modern PSU qualifies.{% endif %}

When to step up a size

Overclocking, a factory-overclocked card, more than four drives or a lot of fans and RGB each add tens of watts. If two or more apply, take the next size up. Check your exact card's spec sheet too — some board partners list a higher minimum.
