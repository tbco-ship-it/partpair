PSU wattage calculator

Select your parts. We add the CPU's rated power (TDP / PBP, manufacturer default), the GPU's board power and about 100 W for the rest of the system, then apply 30% headroom and round up to a common PSU size.

How it sizes the supply

We start from the graphics card's measured gaming power (TechPowerUp's review of that card), add the CPU's rated power and about 75 W for the rest of the system, and keep that sustained load at or under 80% of the label. The card's measured 20 ms spikes must also stay under the label. The safe choice never goes below the card maker's recommended PSU or TechPowerUp's minimum, whichever is higher; where the measured numbers allow it we also show a smaller size that is fine with a good-quality unit. Full rule on the methodology page.

What the estimate leaves out

Heavy overclocking, many hard drives, RGB controllers and pump-driven water cooling each add tens of watts. If your build has several of these, step up one tier.
